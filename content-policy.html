What we do not allow

Counterfeit, replica or reproduction cards offered as genuine. Stolen goods. Listings that misrepresent condition, grading or authenticity. Photographs that are not of the actual item. Harassment, threats, hate speech or sexual content. Impersonating another person or seller. Spam and repeated identical listings. Anything illegal in Israel.

Scams

Asking for payment before any real contact, refusing to meet or to send additional photographs, and pressing you to move to an outside platform immediately are all warning signs. Report them.

How to report

Every listing, profile and conversation has a report action. Reports go to a moderation queue and are never shown to the person reported. You can also block any user. Blocking hides their listings from you, stops them messaging you, and mutes any existing conversation.

What we do about it

We review every report within 24 hours and act on it. Depending on severity we may remove a listing, warn an account, or suspend it permanently. Serious cases may be referred to the police. We do not tolerate people who repeatedly break this policy, and we remove them.

If you disagree with a decision

Write to pokemarketplaceapp@gmail.com and we will look at it again.
