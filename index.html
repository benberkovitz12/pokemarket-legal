Getting help

PokeMarket is a noticeboard for trading cards. If something is not working, or you have a question about your account, write to pokemarketplaceapp@gmail.com and we will reply.

Reporting a listing or a user

Every listing, profile and conversation in the app has a report action. Reports reach a moderation queue and are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block any user from the same menu, which hides their listings and stops them contacting you.

Deleting your account

Settings, then Delete account. This removes your profile, listings and messages. You do not need to contact us to do it.

Safety when trading

No money passes through PokeMarket. Meet in a public place, inspect the card before paying, and be wary of anyone who refuses to meet or pushes you to pay in advance.
