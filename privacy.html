The short version

We collect what the app needs to work, and nothing else. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking services.

What we collect

When you create an account: your email address, and the username and display name you choose. If you choose to add them: a profile photo, a short bio, your city and country, and a WhatsApp number. When you use the app: your listings and their photographs, messages you send to other users, reviews you write, items you add to your watchlist, and reports or blocks you make. Automatically: a count of how many times each of your listings has been viewed, and, if you allow notifications, a device token so we can send them.

What we do not collect

No payment details, because no money passes through PokeMarket. No GPS or precise location: the city on your profile is text you typed, and can be as vague as you like. No contacts, no browsing history, no advertising identifiers.

What other people can see

Public: your username, display name, profile photo, bio, city, your active listings and their photographs, and reviews you have received. Private: your email address, your WhatsApp number, your device token, your messages, your watchlist, and anyone you have blocked or reported. Your WhatsApp number is revealed to a buyer only after you have replied to them, and never otherwise.

Where your data is held

On Supabase, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Listing photographs are stored there and are publicly readable, because listings are shown to people who are not signed in. Do not photograph anything you would not want to be public.

Card information

Card names, set details and card images come from TCGdex, an independent open catalogue. Browsing cards sends no personal information to them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications we store a device token so messages can reach you. Turning notifications off in your device settings clears it, and so does signing out.

How long we keep things

Until you delete them. Deleting a listing removes it and its photographs. Deleting your account removes your profile, listings, photographs and messages. Backups may hold copies for a short period afterwards.

Your rights

You can see and edit most of your data in the app, and delete your account from Settings without asking us. To request a copy of your data, or to ask how it is handled, write to pokemarketplaceapp@gmail.com.

Children

PokeMarket is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.
